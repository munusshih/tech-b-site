## Project 1 Presentation

This week, we’re presenting Project 1: **Epistemological Research of Technology**. We’ll share the final prototypes, observations, and claims, and look back at how each project changed through the four-week process.

## Reflection

After the presentations, reflect on the following questions:

- What did you learn from making a working prototype that you could not learn from research alone?
- What made people read your system as intelligent, responsive, or alive?
- Did your main question change while you were making the project? How?
- What worked, and what didn’t work?
- What would you continue exploring if you had more time?

## Readings

Watch Fei Fei Liu's [**How we teach computers to understand images**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=40riCqvRoMs)(2005), a foundational video in the field of computer vision.

Then, watch Kate Crawford’s [**The Trouble with Bias**](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fMym_BKWQzk)(2018). We will discuss it next week as we begin the next module.

Come ready to discuss:

- How does an image become data that a machine can learn from?
- What role do categories and labels play in training an AI system?
- What kinds of decisions have to be made when building a dataset?
- How might different ways of organizing the same images lead to different outcomes?
- What does these two videos help you notice about the relationship between people, data, and machine learning?
