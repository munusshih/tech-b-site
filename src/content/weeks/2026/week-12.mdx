### Before Workshop 3

You need a local model app and the Qwen2.5 1.5B model on your computer. Choose one option below and complete both steps.

- **Option 1: Llama App (macOS or Windows 11)**
  1. **Download the app.** [Install Llama App](https://llama.app/docs/installation).
  2. **Download the model.** Open the [Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct GGUF page](https://huggingface.co/Qwen/Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct-GGUF) and choose **Use this model → Llama**.
- **Option 2: Ollama (Windows)**
  1. **Download the app.** [Install Ollama](https://ollama.com/download/windows).
  2. **Download the model.** Open PowerShell and run `ollama pull qwen2.5:1.5b`. This downloads the [Qwen2.5 1.5B model](https://ollama.com/library/qwen2.5:1.5b).

Choose one *Alternative Now(s)* sketch from Week 11 and revisit a question that came up during critique. Who might receive, buy, or keep your work? What kind of relationship might emerge from that exchange, and what do you want to explore further?

Building on your sketch, create **three working technical prototypes** using the tools introduced this week: Markov chains, generative grammar with RiTa, and local AI models. You can work with one tool or combine several. Each prototype should be a small program, interface, or script that someone can interact with. They can explore different directions or be iterations of the same idea. They don't need to be polished, but they should run!

As you experiment, consider how different approaches to generating language might change the experience. What can each approach do, where does it fall short, and how might those differences inform your project?

Bring your three prototypes, including your code, sources, rules, prompts, and sample outputs. Be prepared to share what you tried, what you discovered, and where you want to take the project next.
