## Project 2 Presentation

This week, we’re presenting Project 2: **Uncertain Archive**. We’ll share our final works, prototypes, and reflections, and look back at how each project developed through collecting, mapping, experimenting with machine learning, and making.

## Reflection

After the presentations, reflect on the following questions:

- What did you learn from making your archive into an experience for someone else?
- How did your own ways of organizing the archive differ from the machine’s?
- What did the machine make visible, and what did it miss or distort?
- How did your project change as you moved from research and experiments into a design proposal?
- What worked, and what didn’t work?
- What would you continue exploring if you had more time?

## Visit the Exhibition

This week, visit MoMA’s [**Full Disclosure: The Edge of Information Design**](https://www.moma.org/calendar/exhibitions/5926), on Floor 1, 1 South. The exhibition explores how information designers turn data into visual stories, make invisible systems visible, and shape what we understand.

As you look through the exhibition, consider:

- What data or systems does each work make visible?
- How do the designer’s choices shape how you interpret the information?
- What feels clear, uncertain, or left out?
- What connections do you see between the exhibition and your Uncertain Archive project?
